# Product vision

## Problem

Weekly meal planning in a two-person household is often repetitive, fragmented, and hard to keep in sync.

## Vision

Make meal planning collaborative and lightweight: both people can plan meals, manage recipes, and keep one live shopping list without duplicated effort.

## Target users

- Two adults sharing a home and food shopping.
- Users who want practical planning, not a complex nutrition platform.

## Product goals

1. Reduce planning friction each week.
2. Keep shopping aligned with planned meals.
3. Learn from meal feedback (ratings and notes) over time.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Calorie/macro tracking.
- Complex pantry analytics.
- Full social/community recipe platform.
- AI-led planning as a mandatory path.

## Success signals (early)

- Households actively create weekly plans.
- Shopping list completion happens in-app, together.
- Users record feedback after meals, enabling better repeat decisions.
